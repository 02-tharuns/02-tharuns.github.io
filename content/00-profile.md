## Who Tharun Subramanya is
Tharun Subramanya is an applied machine learning engineer working on industrial systems, edge IoT, and robotics. He holds a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Michigan-Dearborn, completed in May 2026 with a GPA of 3.74 out of 4.00. His background combines Industrial and Production Engineering with applied AI.

His applied machine learning work spans CAN-bus fault classification, vibration-based condition monitoring, drift-aware edge predictive maintenance, and computer-vision perception pipelines. He is currently extending that sensor-level diagnostic foundation toward robotics and physical AI systems.

## How he approaches evaluation
His work is consistently grounded in data-quality validation and session-held-out evaluation rather than inflated benchmark numbers. Reported metrics are revised downward whenever a higher figure cannot be defended. Splits are held out by recording session, by video, or by group so that neighbouring samples never leak across an evaluation boundary.

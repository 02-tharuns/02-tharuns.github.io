## WayneHacks 4 hackathon
Third place out of more than 30 teams at WayneHacks 4, hosted by Wayne State University and TechTown Detroit in March 2026. Aligned a three-person cross-functional team from Computer Science and Robotics under a 24-hour constraint, and owned the model pipeline: a nine-feature Random Forest over Wi-Fi channel state information, served through FastAPI and WebSockets. Reached 75.6 percent accuracy and 77.8 percent fall recall. The project is published as GuardWave. During the 24-hour build the team also iterated through a physical sensor prototype, a PIR motion sensor wired to a microcontroller, before converging on the Wi-Fi CSI approach that shipped.

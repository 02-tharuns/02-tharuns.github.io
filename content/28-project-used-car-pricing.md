## Used car price forecasting overview
A price forecasting project over 30,000 Kaggle used-car listings, built November 2023 to January 2024. Earlier applied machine learning work, predating the sensor and edge focus.

## Used car modelling and results
Raised R-squared from 0.68 to 0.80 and cut mean absolute error by 12 percent using stacked regression over Random Forest, gradient boosting and XGBoost. Engineered predictive features including vehicle age, mileage per year and fuel type.

## Used car pipeline and delivery
Built modular scikit-learn pipelines for preprocessing, feature engineering and model training. Delivered interactive dashboards to 8 stakeholders to support pricing decisions.
